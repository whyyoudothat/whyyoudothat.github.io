Privacy Policy

Why You Do That is a short-video channel about psychology and human behavior. The "Why You Do That Bot" is a private desktop tool used only by the channel operator to publish the channel's own videos to YouTube and TikTok through their official APIs.

Who uses the tool

Only the channel operator. The tool is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and is never used on behalf of other people.

What data is processed

  • Platform access tokens issued by YouTube (Google) and TikTok after the operator authorises the tool. They are stored only on the operator's own computer and are used solely to upload videos and read the channel's own statistics.
  • Public channel information returned by the platforms (channel name, video IDs, view counts). This is the channel's own data.

No data about viewers, followers, or any third party is collected, stored, or shared. The tool does not use cookies, trackers, or analytics beyond the platforms' own creator statistics.

Data sharing

Data is sent only to YouTube and TikTok to perform the upload the operator requested. Nothing is sold or passed to anyone else.

Data retention and deletion

Access tokens stay on the operator's computer until the authorisation is revoked. Authorisation can be revoked at any time in the Google account settings ("Third-party apps") or in the TikTok app ("Settings and privacy" → "Security and permissions" → "Apps and services"). Revoking deletes the tool's access immediately; local token files are deleted by the operator.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a comment or message to the channel Why You Do That on YouTube.
